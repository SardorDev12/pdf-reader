
**Full description** (max 4000):
```
Pagemark is a reader that lets you build your own map of what matters in a book, without ever leaving the page.

Read EPUB books and PDFs, and capture as you go:
• Words – long-press a word to save it together with the sentence it appeared in
• Passages – select a few lines, give them a title and find them again later
• Notes – attach your own thoughts to any page or quote
• Bookmarks – mark the page you're on

Everything you save is attached to the page it came from. Swipe in from the left edge to see the words saved on the page you're reading; swipe in from the right edge for that page's passages, notes and bookmarks. Tap any saved item anywhere in the app and Pagemark takes you straight back to the exact spot and highlights it.

Made for students, researchers and anyone who learns from books:
• Works completely offline – no account, no sign-up
• Your library, words, notes and bookmarks stay on your device
• Search across your books, vocabulary, passages and notes
• Resume exactly where you stopped
• Light, sepia and dark reading themes, adjustable text size (EPUB) and zoom (PDF)
• No ads, no tracking

Import books from your device's files. Pagemark doesn't include or sell books; it reads the EPUB and PDF files you add.
```
