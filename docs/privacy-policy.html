Pagemark – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Pagemark ("the app") is an e-book and PDF reader. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your books, notes and reading data stay on your device, and we do not collect them.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • the EPUB and PDF files you choose to add, and their titles, authors and cover images;
  • your reading position and progress;
  • the words, passages, notes and bookmarks you save, with the page they belong to;
  • your display preferences (text size, zoom, theme);
  • simple usage counters (for example how many saved items you reopened) shown to you in Settings.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Because nothing is uploaded, we cannot access, back up or recover it for you. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • The app contains no advertising.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking services, and does not build a profile of you.
  • The app does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • The app only opens files you explicitly pick using Android's file picker.

The one network connection: app updates

The app can receive small updates to its code over the internet using Expo's update service (EAS Update, provided by Expo, expo.dev). When the app checks for an update, a request is sent to Expo's servers that includes technical information needed to choose the right update, such as the app version, platform and update channel, and – as with any internet request – your IP address is visible to the server. None of your books, notes or reading data is included. Expo's handling of this information is described in Expo's privacy policy.

Children

Pagemark is a general-purpose reading tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
